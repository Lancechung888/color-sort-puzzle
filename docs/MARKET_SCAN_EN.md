### Critical takeaway on lids

- **Cosmetic caps** (Mobirix Quest) are common. **Gameplay lids that block pour until uncap are not.**
- Closest listed mechanic is **covered / ice-locked bottles** (unlock by progress or other moves) — different rule, different teach.
- White space for **GOLD LID as a first-class move** remains open in EN stores as of this scan. That is the only durable ASO/UA angle vs Magic Sort / IEC clones.

## 3) Common UA hooks (first 3 seconds)

Genre pattern (Meta / Reels / AppLovin creatives — see [Stack Sort gameplay-hook note](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/imasads_panteons-stack-sort-is-quietly-proving-a-activity-7483869534383161345-_Ube), generic UGC hook practice [Hustler Marketing](https://www.hustlermarketing.com/blog/how-to-write-ugc-ad-hooks-that-stop-the-scroll-on-meta-and-tiktok/)):

1. **Instant pour ASMR** — macro glass + color merge in frame 0; no logo bumper.
2. **Mess → clean** — chaotic board → one clean tube in &lt;3s (satisfaction payoff tease).
3. **Fail / dead-end** — illegal pour shake or jammed board, then undo/hint.
4. **Question overlay (sound-off)** — “Can you solve this?” / “Don’t mess up.”
5. **Twist beat (ColorTube-specific)** — tap pour **blocked by gold lid** → uncap → pour. Contrarian vs “plain water sort.”
6. **Hand / UGC** — finger tapping tubes; works sound-off with bold captions.

**Avoid:** slow title cards, every-level lid spam claims, fake cash-out bait (clone ads poison trust).

## 5) Verdict — title: **ColorTube Sort: Lid Puzzle**

**Keep** (minor ASO support, do not rename to generic Water Sort).

Lid/uncap is real white space vs cosmetic caps and covered-bottle unlocks; dropping “Lid” collapses you into Magic Sort / IEC keyword wars you will lose on budget. Compensate by stuffing **water sort / color sort / tube** into short description + Apple keywords, and force **gold lid in icon + first 3s of preview/UA** (already in store hard rules).

Optional later tweak only if search lift fails: subtitle/short desc lead harder (“Water color sort — lids lock tubes”) without burning the Lid title slot.
