Privacy Policy — ColorTube Sort: Lid Puzzle

Effective date: 2026-09-19

App: ColorTube Sort: Lid Puzzle (Android / iOS)

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes and why. The App is English-first, free to play, may show ads, and offers an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase (remove_ads, target price USD $2.99).

1. Summary

CategoryWhat happens
Account No accounts. We do not ask for name, email, or social login.
Game progress Saved on your device only (e.g. local storage): levels, coins, settings, Remove Ads entitlement flag.
Ads If you have not purchased Remove Ads, Google AdMob may show ads and process device/ad identifiers as described below.
Purchases Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing / Apple StoreKit. We do not receive your full payment card details.
Analytics backend First-party event hooks may log to the device console during development. If we later enable a provider (e.g. Google Analytics / Firebase), this policy will be updated.

2. Information we process

2.1 On-device game data (not uploaded by us)

  • Level progress, stars, coins, hint inventory, daily challenge state, theme unlocks
  • Whether Remove Ads is owned (local flag after a verified purchase / restore)

Clearing app storage or uninstalling the App deletes this on-device data.

2.2 Advertising (AdMob / Google)

When ads are enabled, Google AdMob and related Google services may process, for example:

  • Advertising / device identifiers (e.g. AAID / IDFA where permitted)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP)
  • Device and app information, ad interaction / performance data
  • Diagnostics related to ad delivery and fraud prevention

See Google’s policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and AdMob help for publishers.

Interstitial ads in this App are intended only at natural breaks (e.g. after fail-loop or level clear)— not during an active pour. Rewarded ads are optional (e.g. hints / continue) and rewards are granted only after the reward is earned.

Purchasing Remove Ads turns off interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads may still be offered as a voluntary way to earn in-game benefits unless we state otherwise in a future update.

2.3 Purchases

If you buy Remove Ads or other optional items:

  • Google Play / Apple process the transaction
  • The App verifies entitlement and stores a local owned flag
  • Play Console / App Store Connect retain purchase records under their terms

2.4 What we do not collect

We do not knowingly collect: names, emails, photos, contacts, precise GPS, microphone/camera audio-video, or social graph data—unless a future version adds a feature and this policy is updated first.

3. How we use information

  • Operate and improve the puzzle game
  • Show ads (if not removed) and measure ad performance / prevent fraud
  • Process and restore optional purchases
  • Comply with law and store policies

4. Sharing

  • Google AdMob / Google — advertising and related processing
  • Google Play / Apple — store distribution and billing
  • We do not sell personal information as a consumer-data broker. Advertising partners may receive identifiers as part of serving ads (see their policies).

5. Retention

  • On-device game data: until you clear storage or uninstall
  • Ad / billing data: retained by Google / Apple under their policies
  • We do not operate a separate cloud profile for this App today

6. Your choices

  • Remove Ads (optional IAP) to stop interstitials
  • System settings: limit ad tracking / App Tracking Transparency (iOS) where available
  • EEA/UK and similar regions: consent / privacy options via Google UMP where required
  • Uninstall or clear app data to remove on-device progress

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Do not select “Designed for children” / child-directed treatment for this title unless a future build is redesigned for COPPA/children compliance.

8. International users / legal bases (EEA/UK where applicable)

Where GDPR applies, processing for ads and analytics is typically based on consent and/or legitimate interests in running a free ad-supported game and preventing fraud, as configured in the consent SDK.
